# The journey: from idea to "patent pending"

Seven phases. You can stop after any phase and come back weeks later: run `/patent-kit:start` in your workspace and it resumes from the "Resume here" line of your `tracker.md`.

| Phase | What happens | Command | You end up with | Time | Cost (India) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Orientation** | Learn the few terms that matter, the websites you will use, and the one rule: tell no one publicly until you file | `/patent-kit:start` | This table, the glossary, the websites list | 15 min | Free |
| **1. Capture** | Private workspace and backup; Claude interviews you about the invention | `/patent-kit:start` | `disclosure.md` | 30 to 60 min | Free |
| **2. Prior art** | Searches in batches you approve; what exists, how yours differs | `/patent-kit:prior-art` | `prior-art.md` | 1 to 2 h | Free |
| **3. Draft** | Provisional specification (Form 2), Form 3 and the drawings as compliant PDFs | `/patent-kit:draft`, `/patent-kit:drawings` | PDFs in `filed/` | 2 to 4 h, mostly your review | Free |
| **4. Accounts** | eSign with Aadhaar e-KYC; patent office portal account; email verified. Can be done any time before phase 5 | `/patent-kit:file` | A working login and signature | 30 min | About Rs 250 plus platform charges |
| **5. File** | Form 1, uploads, eSign, payment | `/patent-kit:file` | The receipt with your **application number and priority date** | About 1 h | Rs 1,600 (individual, online, up to 30 pages) |
| **6. After filing** | Deadlines computed; what you may now publish; corrections | `/patent-kit:track` | Calendar dates; "patent pending" | 15 min | Free |

Later, outside this kit's first release:

| When | What | Cost (India) |
|---|---|---|
| Within 12 months (no extension) | Complete specification with claims, Form 5; PCT if you want other countries | Extra pages and claims only |
| Within 31 months | Form 18, request for examination | Rs 4,000 |
| After examination | Reply to objections; hearing; grant; yearly renewals | Varies |
