# India: the websites you will use

In the order you meet them. Costs as seen in October 2026; check on the day.

| When | Website | What you do there | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 2, prior art | **InPASS**, India's patent search: `iprsearch.ipindia.gov.in/PublicSearch` | Search Indian applications and patents | Free |
| Phase 2, prior art | **Google Patents**: `patents.google.com` | Full-text search of patents worldwide | Free |
| Phase 2, prior art | **Espacenet**: `worldwide.espacenet.com` | European Patent Office search, worldwide | Free |
| Phase 2, prior art | **Patentscope**: `patentscope.wipo.int` | PCT international applications | Free |
| Phase 4, before filing | **IP India**: `ipindia.gov.in` | Official rules, current forms, fee schedule, filing process page | Free |
| Phase 4, before filing | **eMudhra eSign**: `e-mudhra.com` (or another licensed eSign or DSC provider; the list is on `cca.gov.in`) | Buy an electronic signature with Aadhaar e-KYC and mobile OTP. A one-year plan of 100 signatures is enough for the whole application | About Rs 250 plus platform charges |
| Phase 4, before filing | **IP India e-filing portal**: `ipronline.ipindia.gov.in` (linked from ipindia.gov.in, Filing Process) | Register with your eSign, verify your email, fill Form 1, upload, sign | Free to register |
| Phase 5, filing | The portal's **payment gateway** (net banking, card or UPI) | Pay the filing fee; get the CBR receipt | Rs 1,600 for an individual, online, up to 30 pages |
| Any time | **ipo-helpdesk@nic.in** | Email the portal help desk (payment debited but not confirmed, portal errors) | Free |
| Phase 6, after filing | The same portal: Form History, Pending CBR, later forms | Check status, file Form 3, Form 18, the complete specification | Per form |

Not needed for an individual: **Startup India / DPIIT** (`startupindia.gov.in`) gives startup fee rates and faster examination only to companies, LLPs and registered partnerships.

Tips:
- Allow pop-ups for `ipronline.ipindia.gov.in`; several buttons open new windows.
- Register on the portal with your name exactly as on the Aadhaar or PAN behind your eSign.
- Never upload PAN, Aadhaar or passport copies to the portal.
